# Privacy Policy — Smart House Backup

**Last updated:** November 23, 2026

Smart House Backup is a personal backup application operated by its owner.

## Information accessed

The application uses Google Drive OAuth authorization to create, read, update, and manage backup files created through the application.

Backup data may include smart home configurations, service settings, application databases, and other information selected by the owner.

## Data storage and security

Backup archives are encrypted locally before being uploaded to the owner's Google Drive account.

OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's Raspberry Pi and are not published on this website.

## Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or intentionally share backup contents with third parties.

## Data retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive account until removed according to the owner's backup retention settings or manually deleted.

## Access revocation

The owner may revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account security settings.

## Contact

Privacy inquiries can be directed to the support email listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
